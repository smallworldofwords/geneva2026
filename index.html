About the Workshop

The Small World of Words (SWOW) project collects free word associations to build large-scale lexical networks across many languages. This international workshop brings together SWOW collaborators from around the world for a full day of exchange, coordination, and scientific discussion.

The workshop features two complementary sessions: a morning of practical collaboration covering data management, participant recruitment, and shared guidelines; and an afternoon of scientific presentations from researchers at the frontier of word association research.

The morning session is held in-person (with Zoom for remote participants); the afternoon is a hybrid open session.
